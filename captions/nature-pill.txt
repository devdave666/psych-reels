Stressed? Twenty minutes outside may help more than you think.

In a 2019 study (MaryCarol Hunter, Brenda Gillespie and Sophie Chen, Frontiers in Psychology), 36 urban adults spent 10 minutes or more in nature at least 3 times a week for 8 weeks. Their stress hormone cortisol fell 21.3% per hour beyond its normal daily drop of 11.7% per hour, and the payoff per minute was greatest at 20 to 30 minutes. It was a small, self-selected sample (92% women), so treat it as a promising nudge, not a prescription.

Today, give yourself 20 minutes with something green. No phone required.
