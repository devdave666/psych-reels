Nervous before something big? Don't try to calm down. Try getting excited.

In a 2014 Harvard Business School study (Alison Wood Brooks, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General), people sang "Don't Stop Believin'" karaoke in front of a stranger and were scored on singing accuracy. Those who said out loud "I am excited" scored about 81%, versus about 69% for saying nothing and about 53% for saying "I am anxious". Their heart rate and reported anxiety did not change, only the label did. It was a short lab task with students, so treat it as a handy mental trick, not a cure.

Next time your heart races, say it out loud: "I am excited." Then go do the thing.
