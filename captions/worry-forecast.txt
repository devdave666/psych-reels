That thing you're dreading? Odds are it never happens.

In a 2020 study (Lucas LaFreniere & Michelle Newman, Behavior Therapy), 29 people with generalized anxiety disorder logged their worries at random times over 10 days, then about 20 days later checked whether each one had actually come true. 91.4% of their worry predictions did not, and the most common result per person was that none of their worries came true. It's a small study of people in an anxiety treatment trial who rated their own outcomes, so treat the exact number as a rough guide, but the pattern is worth testing on yourself.

Write today's worry down, check it in a month, then go do the thing anyway.
