Feel like you never have enough time? Try awe.

In 2012, Melanie Rudd, Kathleen Vohs and Jennifer Aaker (Psychological Science) ran three experiments in which people who felt awe, compared with other emotions like happiness, felt they had more time available, were less impatient, and were more willing to volunteer their time. The authors suggest awe pulls attention into the present, which stretches how time feels. Caveat: a 2019 follow-up found no consistent effect of awe on measured time perception, so this is a promising nudge, not settled science.

Tonight, find something vast (a night sky, a big view) and look up for a minute.
