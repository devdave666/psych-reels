Someone helped you once. Tell them, it will probably land better than you think.

In a 2018 paper (Kumar & Epley, Psychological Science), people wrote gratitude letters and then predicted how the recipients would feel. Writers overestimated how awkward recipients would feel and underestimated how happy and positive they would be. Writers also worried about their own wording, while recipients mostly responded to the warmth of the gesture. Caveat: this reflects what people predicted versus what recipients reported, and press coverage of the paper differs on how many experiments it contained, so we are not quoting numbers.

Who is one person you could thank this week? Send it tonight.
