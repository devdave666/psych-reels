Can't afford to save more right now? Promise your next raise instead.

In a 2004 study (Richard Thaler & Shlomo Benartzi, Journal of Political Economy), employees who said they couldn't save more were offered "Save More Tomorrow": their saving rate would go up automatically at each future pay raise. 78% of those offered joined, 80% of them stayed through the fourth raise, and their average saving rate rose from 3.5% to 13.6% in 40 months.

It was one company and not a randomized trial, but the idea holds up: future you agrees to things present you finds hard.

Pick one thing and commit your next raise, bonus or birthday money to it today.
