Scrolling all night and still feel lonely? Try putting a cap on it.
In a 2018 study (Melissa Hunt, Rachel Marx, Courtney Lipson & Jordyn Young, Journal of Social and Clinical Psychology), 143 University of Pennsylvania undergraduates were randomly assigned either to limit Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat to 10 minutes per app per day, or to use them as usual, for three weeks, with actual use checked from phone screenshots. The limited group reported significantly less loneliness and depression than the usual-use group, and the drop was biggest for people who were more depressed at the start. Honest caveats: it was one group of iPhone-using college students, three apps and three weeks, so treat it as a strong nudge to cut back, not a reason to quit everything.
Tonight, set a 10-minute timer on your most-used app and spend the time you win back with someone real.
