Failed? Being kind to yourself might help you try again.

In a 2012 study (Breines & Chen, Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin), 86 students took a hard vocabulary test, mostly failed it, then got to study for a retake. Those who saw one self-compassionate line ("you're not alone, try not to be too hard on yourself") studied about 51% longer than those who saw no message (306 vs 203 seconds). Against a self-esteem pep-talk the gap was smaller and only borderline, and retake scores did not differ significantly. It is one small lab study, so treat it as a promising clue, not a guarantee.

Next time you slip, talk to yourself like a friend. Then go again.
