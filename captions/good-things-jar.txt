Rough day? Tonight, write down three good things.

In a 2005 online study (Seligman, Steen, Park & Peterson, American Psychologist), 577 adults were randomly given exercises to try. People who wrote down three things that went well each night, plus why they happened, for just one week were happier and less depressed at follow-ups lasting up to six months (411 people finished every follow-up). Honest caveat: it was a self-selected internet sample, and a 2017 re-test (Woodworth et al.) found a placebo exercise improved mood just as much, so treat it as a cheap, low-risk habit, not a cure.

Try it tonight: three things, and why.
