Lying awake running through tomorrow? Put it on paper.

In a 2018 sleep-lab study, Scullin and colleagues at Baylor had 57 healthy young adults write for 5 minutes before bed. Half listed what they had to do over the next few days, half listed what they had already done. The to-do group fell asleep in about 16 minutes vs 25 (roughly 9 minutes faster), and the more specific the list, the faster they fell asleep.
Honest caveats: it was one night, in a lab, with people who didn't have sleep problems, and there was no "no writing" group, so treat it as a promising, cheap habit, not a cure for insomnia.

Tonight, take 5 minutes and write tomorrow's list, as specific as you can. What's the first thing going on yours?
