You're not starting from zero.

In a classic study (Nunes & Drèze, 2006), a car wash handed out loyalty cards. Every card needed the same eight washes, but some came with two free stamps already on them. 34% of those cards were finished, versus 19% of the blank ones.

Feeling like you've already begun makes you keep going. So before you decide you're behind, count what you've already done.
