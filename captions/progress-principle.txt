Think of your best day at work. What made it great?

In a diary study of about 12,000 daily entries from 238 professionals in 26 project teams (Amabile & Kramer, 2011, The Progress Principle), making progress in meaningful work was involved in 76% of people's best days. It was self-reported and correlational, and the sample was mostly college-educated knowledge workers, so it shows a pattern, not proof of cause.

Today, pick one small step forward and take it.
