How long does it take to build a habit?

In one UCL study, the median was 66 days before a new daily behaviour felt automatic. The range was huge: 18 to 254 days. Missing a single day did not derail the process.

Small sample (39 people with usable data), so treat 66 as a rough guide, not a deadline.

Lally et al., 2010, European Journal of Social Psychology.
