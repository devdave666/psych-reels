Only daydreaming about your goal? Add the obstacle, then a plan.

In a 2011 study, Duckworth, Grant, Loew, Oettingen and Gollwitzer had 66 high-school students either write a 30-minute exercise (picture the goal, name the obstacle, make an if-then plan) or do a neutral writing task. The exercise group completed more than 60% more practice questions for their PSAT exam. It's a small study, but it fits earlier work by Oettingen showing that purely positive fantasies can sap effort.

Tonight: write your wish, the one thing most likely to stop you, and "if that happens, then I will..."
