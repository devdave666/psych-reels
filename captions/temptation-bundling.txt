Dreading it? Pair it with something you love.

In a 2014 study (Milkman, Minson & Volpp, Management Science), 226 people were randomly assigned to hear gripping audiobooks only at the gym, or not. Early on, the group locked into gym-only listening visited 51% more often than the control group, and a looser "try to listen at the gym" group visited 29% more. The effect faded over the nine weeks, especially after Thanksgiving, so treat it as a boost to get you started, not a permanent fix.

Pick one thing you should do and attach one thing you love to it, only while you do it.
