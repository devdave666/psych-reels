Blew your week? You don't have to wait for a perfect moment, just a new one.

In a 2014 study (Dai, Milkman & Riis, Management Science), researchers looked at card-reader data from a university gym. Compared with a normal day, the chance a student went to the gym was 47% higher at the start of a semester and 33% higher at the start of a week. They saw the same pattern in Google searches for "diet" and in goal commitments. It is observational data from one university gym, so it shows a pattern, not proof of cause.

Treat the next Monday, the 1st of the month or your birthday as a clean slate, then use it to start.
