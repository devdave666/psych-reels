Stressed before something big? Try talking to yourself by name.

In a 2014 paper (Kross et al., Journal of Personality and Social Psychology), seven studies with 585 people in total tested "self-talk" under social stress, such as giving a speech. People who referred to themselves by their own name or as "you" instead of "I" showed less distress, and objective raters judged their performance as better. The effect did not depend on how socially anxious people were, though these were short lab tasks, so it is a useful mental trick rather than a cure.

Next time your head is loud, zoom out: "Why is [your name] nervous?" Then take the first step.
