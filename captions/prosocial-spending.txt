Got a spare $5? Try spending it on someone else.

In a 2008 study (Dunn, Aknin & Norton, Science), 46 students were given $5 or $20 to spend by the end of the day, either on themselves or on someone else. Those who spent it on others reported being happier that evening, and the amount ($5 vs $20) made no difference. It's a small study: a 2022 close replication (133 people, $10) found no significant effect on the original analysis, only on an exploratory measure, so treat this as a promising idea to test, not a guarantee.
Try it today: spend $5 on someone else and notice how your day feels.
